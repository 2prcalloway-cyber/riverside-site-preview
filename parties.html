Book in minutes

Small party? Pick your time and book online.

Up to 50 guests with tables and chairs set up for you, or up to 80 with seating only. Guests clean up after themselves, and there’s a playground for the little ones.

  • 2 hours to a full day (weekends: 3-hour minimum)
  • Barn events up to 50: tables, chairs & setup included
  • Up to 80 guests with seating only inside the barn, per the fire marshal
  • Over 50 guests and need tables and chairs set up outside? Add a flat $75
  • Dedicated playground area
  • Free 45-minute setup and 45-minute cleanup; guests clean up after themselves
  • Book up to 90 days ahead. Saturdays are mainly for weddings, but open Saturdays inside 90 days are welcome. Just ask.
  • Add-ons: linens $10 per table (partial length) or $15 (floor length); tents $450 for one, $700 for both
  • Payment: half at booking, the other half 15 days before your party
  • Reschedule 30 or more days out and your full booking payment is credited to the new date; less than 30 days out, half of your booking payment is credited
