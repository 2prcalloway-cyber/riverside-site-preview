Deposits & payments

Reserving your date

Party payments: Half is due at booking, and the other half 15 days before your party date.

Party reschedules: If you reschedule 30 or more days before your date, your full booking payment is credited to the new date. Less than 30 days out, half of your booking payment is credited.

Wedding deposit: Half of your wedding total reserves your date.

Payment schedule: The other half is due 30 days before your wedding.

Cancellations & rescheduling: Wedding deposits are non-refundable. If you cancel at least 60 days before your date and choose a new date, we’ll apply half of your deposit to the new date.

Pricing questions

Before you ask

How do we reserve our date? What’s the deposit?

For weddings, half is due to reserve your date, and the other half is due 30 days before your wedding.

For parties, half is due at booking, and the other half 15 days before your party date.

What’s your cancellation / reschedule policy?

Wedding deposits are non-refundable. If you cancel at least 60 days before your date and choose a new date, we’ll apply half of your deposit to the new date.

Parties: if you reschedule 30 or more days before your date, your full booking payment is credited to the new date. Less than 30 days out, half of your booking payment is credited.

Can we tour first?

Absolutely. Tours are free. Book a time online or call/text 336-270-9860.
