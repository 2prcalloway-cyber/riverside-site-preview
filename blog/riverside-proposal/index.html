New at RiversideSept 30, 2026By Paula & Penny

The Riverside Proposal: Say Yes at Riverside

A porch dinner or a picnic by the river, set up and waiting for the two of you. All you have to bring is the ring.

Some questions deserve a beautiful place to be asked. Our field, our river and our porch are some of our very favorite spots on the farm, so we put together a way for you to share one of them with the person you love on the day you ask them to marry you.

We call it The Riverside Proposal. You pick the setup, we get everything ready ahead of time, and then we slip away and leave the moment to the two of you.

Two ways to ask

Setup one
Porch Dinner
$375

One table on the tap room porch, decorated just for the two of you and lit with battery candlelight. We pick up a really nice dinner for two from a restaurant in King and have it waiting at your table.

Includes dinner for two from a local King restaurant, up to $90 in food and drinks before tax and tip. Choose from our featured menu, or go over the allowance and we’ll just add the difference to your total.

Setup two
Riverside Picnic
$295

A picnic out in the field or down by the river, with a cozy blanket, pillows, a picnic basket and lanterns. Relaxed and simple, surrounded by the outdoors.

Includes a picnic spread for two, up to $60 in food and drinks before tax and tip. Want something extra? We’ll add any amount over the allowance to your total.

Couples choose one setup. Both setups are for 2 hours and can be booked up to 30 days in advance. See all our options on the Pricing page.

How it works

  1. Call or text usto pick your date and choose the Porch Dinner or the Riverside Picnic.
  2. We handle the setup,from the table or blanket to the candles, lanterns and your meal.
  3. You bring your someone specialout to the farm at the time we plan together.
  4. We give you your privacyand clean everything up after you’ve gone, so you can just enjoy the night.
Picnic & a Promise

We’ll quietly snap a few photos of the big moment for you. It’s included with both setups. Want to add a little extra? Our Flowers & Sign add-on is $75.

However you picture the moment, we’d be honored to help set the scene. And if the answer is yes, we’d love to show you around the rest of the farm, too.

With love, Paula & Penny
